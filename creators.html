Key facts

What it is
ApexYard gives your agents a team's process, then checks every pull request against it.
Keel
Your team's rules and context, read by every agent.
Three products
Build guides the agent. Gate checks each pull request. Readiness measures a whole repository.
Works with
Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Pi and omp.
Skills
69 slash commands. See the skills page.
Licence
The framework is open source under the MIT licence.
