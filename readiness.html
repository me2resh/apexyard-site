What Readiness does

Ten areas in one audit

We run the readiness audit on your repository. You get a go or no-go, the gaps in each area and the top actions. It covers security, accessibility, compliance, analytics, search, AI search, performance, monitoring, documentation and behaviour quality.

A readiness score for every repository

Each repository gets a score and a trend over time. The audit runs on a schedule, every week.

Runs in your own CI

The audit runs in your CI with your own model key. Only scores and findings reach the dashboard.

How it connects

Measures each repository against Keel, and turns repeated gaps into new rules.

Turn a finding into a rule, and your agents pick it up on their next ticket in Build.

A score tells you what is wrong. The Gate stops it happening again.
