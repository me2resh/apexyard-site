What Readiness does

Eight audits in one run

Security, threat model, accessibility, performance, monitoring, compliance, documentation and test quality, across the whole system rather than one diff.

A score, a verdict and the next three fixes

Each repository gets a Production-Ready Score and a go or no-go. Coming A trend shows whether it is improving, and the top actions say what to do next.

Your code stays in your CI

The audit runs in your own CI with your own model key. Only scores and findings reach the dashboard. Runs on a schedule, every night or every week. Coming Or run it whenever you ask.

How it connects

A finding becomes a rule in one step. Build teaches it to your agents, and the Gate enforces it on the next merge.
