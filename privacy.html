This notice tells you what data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

What we collect and why

We collect data in four ways. Each part below tells you what we collect, why, the lawful basis under UK GDPR, and how long we keep it.

Analytics, only after you accept

What
We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and the pages that people view. The Google tag and Google Analytics load only after you press Accept in the cookie banner. Before that, the site sends nothing to Google.
Why
To learn which pages people use, so that we can make the site better.
Lawful basis
Your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

Waitlist answers

What
The waitlist page does not send your answers to us. When you press the button, the page shows an email link that holds your answers. The email address that you type on the page is not in that email. If you send the email from your own email program, we receive your answers and the email address that you send from.
Why
To choose who joins next, and to reply to you.
Lawful basis
Our legitimate interests in replying to your request. Where you ask to use the product, the steps that you ask for before a contract.
How long
12 months after our last contact with you. We delete it sooner if you ask.

Demo and call bookings

What
“Book a demo”, “Book a readiness call” and “Book an assessment” open the booking service that we use. That service collects the details that you give it, and sends the booking to us. The privacy notice of that service also applies.
Why
To arrange the demo or the call, and to reply to you.
Lawful basis
Our legitimate interests in replying to your request. Where relevant, the steps that you ask for before a contract.
How long
We keep booking emails for 12 months after our last contact with you. We delete them sooner if you ask.

Settings in your browser

What
The site keeps settings in the storage of your browser. On the product pages: ay-consent (your cookie choice) and ay-theme (light or dark), in local storage. On the /docs pages: starlight-theme (light or dark), in local storage, and the open and closed parts of the side menu, in session storage. These settings stay in your browser. The site does not send them to us.
Why
To remember your own choices.
Lawful basis
They are necessary to remember the choices that you made.
How long
Local storage: until you change the choice or clear the site data in your browser. Session storage: until you close the browser tab.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies only after you press Accept.

You can change your choice at any time. The button below shows the cookie banner again and turns analytics storage off until you choose. To remove cookies that Google Analytics set before, clear the cookies for this site in your browser.

Who receives your data

  • Our analytics provider (Google), only after you accept analytics.
  • Our hosting provider and content delivery network, which send the pages to your browser.
  • The image service that shows the GitHub star and fork counts on the home page. Your browser loads these images from that service on each visit to the home page, so it receives your IP address.
  • The booking service that we use, when you book a demo or a call.

Transfers outside the UK

Google and the other services in the list above can process data outside the United Kingdom. Such transfers use safeguards that UK law accepts.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data (access);
  • correct your data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • object to how we use your data;
  • get your data in a format that you can move (portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use a right, email us. To withdraw consent for analytics, use the button in the Cookies part above.

Complaints

Please contact us first. We want to fix the problem. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.
