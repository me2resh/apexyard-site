Reports and assessments

Assessments: details to follow.

What a review gives you

  1. The audit. We run the readiness audit on your repository. It checks ten areas, from security to behaviour quality.
  2. The report. You get a written report: a go or no-go verdict, the gaps in each area and the top actions.
  3. The call. We take you through the report and answer your questions.
